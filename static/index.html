How to read ThreatPulse

P1 Patch now
Attackers are already using it (CISA confirmed), or ransomware gangs are.
P2 Patch this week
Not confirmed yet, but EPSS says there's a 50%+ chance of attacks within 30 days.
P3 Plan it
Serious (CVSS 7+), no sign of attacks yet.
Exploited (CISA KEV)
CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities list: flaws with evidence of real attacks. "Due" is the deadline US agencies must patch by — a good benchmark for anyone.
EPSS
Exploit Prediction Scoring System: the probability this flaw is exploited in the next 30 days.
CVSS
How bad the flaw is in theory, 0–10. High CVSS without attacks is less urgent than a lower score that's being exploited.
✦ Claude summary
A short plain-English explanation written by Claude (Anthropic's AI) from the official advisory. Helpful, but it can be wrong — the official description and advisory link are always under "Details".
★ My stack
Type the vendors you use. Matching items are starred and shown first. Stored only in your browser.
Leak-site claim
A ransomware gang posted the organisation on its extortion site. "2 trackers" means two independent trackers saw it.
Dark-web leak report
A write-up by researchers who watch criminal forums, saying someone posted or is selling an organisation's data. It's a claim until the organisation confirms it. "Confirmed" items are verified breaches from Have I Been Pwned.
IOC
Indicator of compromise: a malicious URL, domain, IP or file hash. Shown defanged (hxxp, [.]) so it can't be clicked by accident — copy into your blocklists.
Outage detector
Reads each provider's official status page every hour, and checks your own sites for uptime, speed and TLS certificate expiry.
Keyboard
Press / to search. Each section has its own link, e.g. #outages, to share.
